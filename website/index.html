ABOUT

Start tiny.
Keep building.

SimpleAI's Studio is an experiment in building artificial intelligence models from the ground up.

Models begin with random weights and are trained specifically for their own tasks instead of relying on downloaded pretrained models or external AI APIs.

The goal is simple: start ridiculously small, learn what works, and gradually build larger and more capable models.
